How it works

Fill: choose a color, then tap any area of the picture. Areas are the spaces between the black lines.

Pick: tap an area to borrow its color (or hold Alt while tapping).

Split: when two parts run together because a line has a gap (like hair into sky), start inside the part to change and draw a loop around just that part.

Show guessed areas stripes every area the computer colored by guessing from its neighbor. These are the ones most worth checking.

Hover to see each area outlined. Scroll with the mouse wheel; hold Ctrl and scroll to zoom.

Save writes your choices into the page's color recipe and rebuilds it. Changes reach the iPad after the next push to GitHub.
